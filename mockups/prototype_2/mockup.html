LIFESTYLE CODEX // DAILY DISCIPLINE

The Climate Action Codex

Eleven non-negotiable operational protocols organized across Energy, Water, Waste, and Consumption vectors.

ENERGY WATER WASTE CONSUMPTION
PROTOCOL 01 VECTOR // ENERGY

Power Conservation

Switch off lights, fans, and electronics when leaving a room. Never let unoccupied spaces drain campus energy grids.

PROTOCOL 02 VECTOR // ENERGY

Vampire Power Cutoff

Unplug idle chargers, adapters, and appliances when not in active use to eradicate passive electrical hemorrhage.

PROTOCOL 03 VECTOR // WATER

Water Valve Discipline

Turn off the tap completely while brushing teeth or shaving. Protect Bangalore's strained groundwater reserves.

PROTOCOL 04 VECTOR // WASTE

Reusable Vessel Mandate

Carry a personal reusable steel bottle, cloth tote bag, and meal container everywhere instead of disposable single-use items.

PROTOCOL 05 VECTOR // WASTE

Organic Bio-Composting

Compost vegetable peels, tea leaves, and organic food scraps in aerated home or hostel bins rather than sending them to landfills.

PROTOCOL 06 VECTOR // CONSUMPTION

Zero Food Waste

Plan daily meals, order strictly what you will consume, and utilize clean leftovers to systematically eliminate food waste.

PROTOCOL 07 VECTOR // CONSUMPTION

Circular Item Renewal

Repair damaged textiles, reuse containers, or donate usable surplus to peer collectives instead of purchasing brand-new goods.

PROTOCOL 08 VECTOR // WATER & ENERGY

Appliance Batch Cycles

Run washing machines and dishwashers exclusively on full loads to maximize electrical and hydraulic efficiency.

PROTOCOL 09 VECTOR // WASTE

Packaging Refusal

Decline extra commercial packaging, single-use plastic cutlery, unnecessary printed receipts, and promotional marketing collateral.

PROTOCOL 10 VECTOR // WASTE

Upcycled Glass Storage

Clean and repurpose glass condiment jars and sturdy containers for pantry food storage, pen holders, and desktop terrariums.

PROTOCOL 11 VECTOR // RESOURCE CONSERVATION

Dual-Sided Digital Transition

Print strictly double-sided when academic submissions mandate physical copies, and embrace a 100% digital-first notes policy across university classrooms.
